My College Journey

My journey in college has been an incredibly joyful and meaningful experience, filled with personal growth and moments that pushed my limits. Along the way, I met amazing people who I never expected would become my lifelong friends. College also challenged me to step out of my comfort zone, especially in learning how to be a firm and decisive leader when directing projects.

During my first year, I joined various student organizations simply to explore my interests and discover who I was. To my suprise, it did not take long before I was trusted with the role of Chairman. Now in my second year, I have taken on an even greater responsibility as the Vice president for External Affairs od our organization.

Balancing these leadership roles alongside my academic workload has truly taught me the value of discipline, adaptability, and resilience. Representing our organization to outside partners has expanded my vision for what I can achieve, proving to me that taking risks early on was worth every effort. Looking ahead, I am excited to carry these experiences, friendships, and leadership lessons into the future as I continue to move closer to my goals.
